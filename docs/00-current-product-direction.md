# Current product direction

Source: founder handover supplied 6 September 2026. This takes precedence over older numbered design/spec documents and HTML exports, which remain historical references.

## Confirmed

Soul Tribe helps adults find meaningful real-world friendships through self-understanding, compatibility, concrete Pitches, host curation, outings and private reflection. Continued friendship beyond the app is the goal.

Keep Social Signature (self profile), Tribal Pass (progressive assessment) and Connection Notes (pair explanation) distinct. Bonds describe observed continuation, not a compatibility prediction. Preserve old database names where changing them adds migration risk.

The baseline in this handover is eight questions. Save exact answers privately, support editing, and keep sensitive information optional. Desired qualities in friends are not the author's personal values. Avoid diagnostic, mystical and deterministic claims; missing evidence stays unknown. Numeric ranking stays internal to the experience.

Outings navigation is Your Pitches, Invited, Confirmed and Past, with a prominent new-Pitch action. Backend event states and membership states remain separate. Host approval and invitee acceptance must be enforced outside the browser. Messages require shared accepted outing context. Exact logistics belong behind the same access boundary; MVP venues are public.

Blocking quietly suppresses discovery in both directions. Reflections and continuation answers are private. Neither participation counts nor public star ratings establish personal trust.

Visual direction: Warm Bone, Forest, Moss, Sage, Sand, Stone and Muted Clay; warm, calm, legible, spacious. Avoid excessive tribal language, dashboard overload, dark glass decoration and dating cues.

## Open product decisions

Final resonance labels, matching weights, behavior-learning policy, exact individual/community capacities, invitation reservations, waitlists, verification, demographics policy, venue reveal rules beyond the initial restriction, Standing presentation and monetisation are not locked.

Current implementation hypotheses must be labeled and configurable. Do not impose a subscription wall, hard-code permanent capacities or add partner data sales. Payments and entitlements can be extended later.

## Historical contradictions

Older docs require six-person free-tier caps, fixed weights, universal friction, an illustrated world on every screen and a persistent artifact for every feature. Those requirements have been superseded by this brief. Existing old copy or formulas describe implementation history, not confirmation from the founder.
